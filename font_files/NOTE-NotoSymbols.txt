NotoSymbols.ttf
===============
Merged secondary symbol fallback (used after DejaVuSubset for symbols DejaVu lacks).

Built deterministically by tools/build_noto_symbols.py from three OFL Noto fonts
(pinned by SHA-256 in that script):
  - Noto Sans Symbols   (variable, instantiated at wght=400)
  - Noto Sans Symbols 2  (Regular)
  - Noto Sans Math       (Regular)

Each source is reduced to glyph-outline tables only, merged, then subset to
codepoints >= U+2000 (Latin-free -> cannot poison a hand-drawn run in resvg).
~5467 symbol glyphs.
